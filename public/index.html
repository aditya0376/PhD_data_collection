Information and Consent

You are invited to take part in a research study conducted by Mr. Aditya Man Borborah, Assistant Professor, Assam Women's University, as part of doctoral research at the Centre for Management Studies, North Eastern Regional Institute of Science and Technology (NERIST).

What the study is about. This study examines how parents respond to different kinds of information printed on packaging for children's products, and how that information affects their buying decisions.

What you will be asked to do. You will answer some questions about yourself and your shopping habits, read a short description of a product label, and then answer questions about your reactions to it. The survey takes about 18 to 20 minutes.

Voluntary participation. Your participation is entirely voluntary. You may skip any question or stop at any point without giving a reason and without any consequence.

Confidentiality. Your responses are anonymous. No information that could identify you will be collected or stored. Data will be reported only in aggregate form and will be used solely for academic research and publication. Data will be retained only as long as required for the research and then securely deleted, in line with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

Risks and benefits. There are no foreseeable risks to participating. There is no payment for participation.

Ethics approval. This study has been approved by the Institutional Research Ethics Committee, NERIST [approval reference: ____________].

Questions. For any questions about this research, contact: aditya@awu.ac.in (email) / +91-9707156777 (phone).
